The Problem

  • Long waits for towing and informal roadside support.
  • No real-time visibility into responder location or ETA.
  • Unclear provider quality and inconsistent communication.

The DriveMate Solution

  • Immediate matching with nearby service providers.
  • Live map-based tracking through a mobile-first workflow.
  • Ratings, history, and provider-side tools for better accountability.

High-Performance Features

  • Real-time GPS location detection and provider discovery.
  • 2km smart matching for rapid service dispatch.
  • Emergency SOS flow for urgent roadside breakdowns.
  • Google Maps-based tracking and route visibility.
  • Provider dashboard for handling incoming requests.
  • History, ratings, and admin-level oversight for service quality.

How It Works

The flow is deliberately simple so a driver can request help under stress with minimal friction.

1. Launch the App

DriveMate captures the current location and prepares nearby service discovery.

2. Trigger SOS

The request is sent to providers in range with the relevant incident context.

3. Track the Provider

The driver sees the mechanic movement and progress on the map in real time.

4. Complete and Review

The service is resolved, recorded, and available for feedback and history.
